Brightness isn’t how bright your TV is

On many TVs, the control called Brightness doesn’t make the picture brighter. It decides where black sits. The control that makes the whole picture brighter usually has another name.

Three controls, three jobs

Try the sliders above. Black level moves the dark end of the scale. Backlight turns the whole thing up and down without changing which steps you can tell apart.

Black level
Where black sits. Set it once with a test pattern and leave it.
Backlight or light output
How bright the whole picture is. Change it to suit the room: brighter by day, dimmer at night.
Contrast
Where white sits. Too high, and the brightest details merge into flat white.

Your TV may use different names

The black-level control has a different name on almost every brand:

  • Samsung
    Shadow Detail
  • LG
    Screen Brightness
  • Sony
    Black level
  • TCL
    Black Level
  • Hisense
    Brightness
