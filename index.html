TV not found?

Chrome must allow this site to find devices on your local network. Use the button above, or tap the icon left of the web address → Site settings → Local network access → Allow.

Make sure the phone and the Chromecast are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network, and not mobile data).

On Android 12 or later, Chrome needs the Nearby devices permission: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Nearby devices → Allow. Then reload this page.

Check that casting works at all: open the YouTube app and look for its Cast icon. If YouTube can’t find the TV either, the issue is the network or the Chromecast, not this page.
